SEMINAR 03

Understanding Artificial Intelligence: A Beginner's Guide to AI Concepts and Real-World Applications

Clarendon College Inc. · Online via Microsoft Teams · August 19, 2026

Narrative Report

The seminar introduced me to the fundamental concepts and development of Artificial Intelligence. I learned how AI can imitate certain human abilities, such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, interpreting images, and extracting information. The discussion also covered different AI workloads, including generative AI, computer vision, computer speech, and language processing, helping me understand that AI has applications beyond chatbots.

Another key takeaway was learning about AI agents, which can use models, instructions, tools, and knowledge to perform tasks. The seminar also presented AI applications in business, education, healthcare, and manufacturing. These discussions helped me appreciate how AI can support automation and decision-making, motivating me to continue learning about its applications as an IT student.
