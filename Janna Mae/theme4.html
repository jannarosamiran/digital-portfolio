BlackStride Cyber · Online via Microsoft Teams · August 14, 2026

Narrative Report

The seminar refreshed my understanding of Artificial Intelligence and its role in modern technology. I learned about different AI workloads, including generative AI, language processing, computer speech, computer vision, and information extraction. It also explained how machine learning supports these capabilities by allowing systems to learn from data and perform various tasks.

The discussion also introduced AI agents that use models, instructions, tools, and knowledge to complete tasks rather than simply provide information. I gained a better understanding of how AI can be integrated into Microsoft Azure and modern cloud environments. Overall, the seminar helped me recognize how AI and cloud technology can work together to support digital services and applications across different industries.
