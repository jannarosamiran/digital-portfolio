Reach Beyond Limits: Creating Accessible Social Media Content

LIFT Oraganization Inc. · Online via Zoom · August 15, 2026

Narrative Report

The webinar “Reach Beyond Limits: Creating Accessible Social Media Content” discussed how to make digital content accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. I learned the importance of using proper alt text to describe images and help screen reader users understand visual information.

As an IT student, I realized that accessibility should be considered when developing websites, applications, and social media content. The webinar taught me that simple practices can make digital information more inclusive and easier for everyone to access.
