SEMINAR 01

Cultivating Innovation: Turning Agricultural Challenges into Tech Opportunities

DICT CALABARZON REGIONAL OFFICE · Online via Zoom · August 27, 2026

Narrative Report

The webinar helped me understand the challenges faced by Philippine farmers, including limited access to technology and infrastructure, fragmented landholdings, and limited market opportunities. I learned about sustainable agriculture and the importance of understanding farmers' actual needs before developing technological solutions. The discussion also introduced design thinking, agricultural technology, and agri-tech startups as ways to encourage innovation in the agricultural sector.

Based on my learnings, IT professionals can contribute by developing smart farming systems, monitoring tools, digital platforms, and data-driven solutions to help farmers improve productivity and reduce losses. The seminar showed me that technology can address problems beyond traditional computing environments. It encouraged me to consider how my knowledge and skills in IT could be used to develop practical solutions that benefit farmers, communities, and the environment.
