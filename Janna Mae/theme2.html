SEMINAR 02

CyberNet: Mastering Networking and Cyber Defense

West Visayas State University · Online via Google Meet · September 12, 2026

Narrative Report

The seminar helped me understand network security risks, particularly how a compromised device in a flat network can affect other connected systems. I learned important security principles such as Assume Breach, Question Everything, Least Privilege, Defense in Depth, and Zero Trust Architecture. These concepts emphasized the importance of being proactive in protecting networks rather than waiting for security incidents to happen.

The discussion also highlighted network segmentation, access control, and continuous monitoring as ways to prevent unauthorized access and minimize the impact of security breaches. Based on these learnings, I realized that IT systems should have multiple layers of protection. These practices can also be applied to school and community networks to protect users, devices, and sensitive information.

SEMINAR 02

Don’t Get Hacked: Detect, Defend, and Secure Your Digital Life

DICT REGION V-CATANDUANES PROVINCIAL OFFICE · Online via Google Meet · August 13, 2026

Narrative Report

The webinar taught me the importance of protecting personal information and recognizing common online scams. I learned that sharing too much information online can expose people to scams, identity theft, and unauthorized access. The discussion encouraged me to review privacy settings, avoid suspicious links and surveys, and carefully check the permissions requested by applications.

I also learned the importance of using strong and unique passwords, keeping account credentials private, and enabling multi-factor authentication. These practices reminded me to be more responsible when managing my online accounts and sharing information. I realized that simple digital safety habits can help protect not only individuals but also the wider community from cyber threats.
